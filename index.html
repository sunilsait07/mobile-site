CODE P0301 Toyota RAV4

Cylinder 1 Misfire

Customer reported rough idling and flashing Check Engine Light on their driveway.

Resolution:
Mechanic swapped coils to verify fault, then replaced cylinder 1 ignition coil and spark plug. Cleared codes and verified repair.
Time Saved: 3.5 Hours Saved Towing Fee
CODE P0420 Honda Civic

Catalytic Converter Efficiency

Check Engine Light illuminated before state emissions inspection at office parking.

Resolution:
Tested downstream O2 sensor voltages and exhaust leaks. Found faulty rear O2 sensor giving false readings. Sensor replaced.
Time Saved: 4 Hours Passed Emissions
CODE P0455 Ford Explorer

EVAP Gross Leak Detected

Warning light active. Customer wanted diagnostics before driving on a long road trip.

Resolution:
Performed smoke test on fuel vapor lines. Discovered loose fuel cap seal and a minor crack in EVAP canister purge valve.
Time Saved: 2 Hours Trip Ready
