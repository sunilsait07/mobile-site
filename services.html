Our Process

How We Diagnose Your Vehicle

A step-by-step look at our professional diagnostic process.

1. Schedule Your Appointment

Book online or call us. Choose your preferred date, time, and location. We come to you — home, office, or anywhere.

2. Mechanic Arrives

Your certified mechanic arrives at your location with professional-grade OBD2 equipment and tools.

3. Connect & Scan

We connect our scanner to your vehicle's OBD2 port and run a comprehensive diagnostic scan of all systems.

4. Analyze Results

Our expert analyzes the diagnostic data, interprets trouble codes, and assesses the severity of any issues found.

5. Deliver Report & Estimates

You receive a detailed digital report with all findings, plain-language explanations, and transparent repair estimates.

Preparation

Pre-Scan Vehicle Checklist

Help our technician complete your diagnostics efficiently by preparing these few items before arrival.

1. Ensure Vehicle Access

Park your car in a safe, flat location (driveway, street, or parking spot) with at least 3 feet of clearance around the driver-side door with space.

2. Have Keys & Fob Ready

The mechanic will need the ignition keys to power on the dashboard electronics during scanner connection (the engine may remain off).

3. Clear Dashboard Area

Please clear any personal items from the driver’s footwell area, as the OBD2 diagnostic port is typically located beneath the steering column.
